Why We Exist

The talent abroad is real, skilled, and available. What goes wrong is everything around the hire. Companies pick a name off a list, write a one-line job description, skip onboarding, and expect a stranger to read their mind. A few months later they decide offshore does not work.

It was never the person. It was the process. Recruiting pipelines that were never built, payroll no one set up, compliance no one checked, and management no one owned. Fix those, and the model holds up. Skip them, and no amount of cost savings makes up the difference.

So we took the whole operational load off the table. You describe the role and pick your hire. We handle sourcing, testing, payroll, compliance, and day-to-day management. You get a team member who works like any other, and you get your week back.
