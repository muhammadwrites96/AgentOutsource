Meet Our Founder

I’m Ghulam Mustafa, founder of Agent Outsource. Over the past 8+ years, I’ve been working in the outsourcing and offshore talent space, helping businesses build and manage high-performing teams.

Over the years, I’ve had the opportunity to work with businesses across different industries and understand what it really takes to build a reliable offshore team.

There are two things I’ve learned that make the biggest difference: operational excellence and the right talent.

We focus on building repeatable processes, finding the right people, and creating offshore teams that become a true extension of our clients’ businesses, not just an outsourced resource.

The goal is simple: help companies access great talent, scale faster, reduce operational costs, and build teams they can rely on.

If you’re a CEO, founder, or C-level executive looking to hire offshore talent, I’d love to connect.
