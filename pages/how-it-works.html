The Process

How It Works

You are only involved where your judgment matters. Everything else is our job.

Step 01

Tell Us the Role

We get clear on the tasks, tools, hours, and what a great hire looks like a few months in. The sharper the brief, the sharper the shortlist.

Step 02

We Build Your Shortlist

We source far wider than any single job post, screen for skills, English, and tool fluency, then cut hard. Finalists take a paid test on real work from your role.

Step 03

You Choose

You interview a small group of people who have already proven they can do the job. You make the final decision, not us.

Step 04

We Keep It Running

Onboarding, payroll, compliance, and ongoing management stay with us for as long as your hire is on the team.

Clear Lines

Who Owns What

No grey areas. You lead the work. We carry the operational load around it.

You Own

  • → Defining the role and priorities
  • → Interviewing and approving the hire
  • → Day-to-day direction and feedback
  • → The final call on everything that matters

We Own

  • → Sourcing, screening, and testing
  • → Payroll in every country we recruit from
  • → Compliance and local employment law
  • → Onboarding, retention, and account management
Vetting

How We Test Candidates

By the time you meet someone, they have already been tested for your role. Every stage is built around your specific job.

01

Screen

English fluency, clarity, call quality, and a first look at relevant experience.

02

Preview Test

How they think through the real problems your role involves.

03

Deep Interview

Experience, past performance, and culture fit, in depth.

04

Paid Work Test

A few hours of the actual tasks they would do for you. We pay them for it.

The other side of it

If the match isn't right, that's on us

Testing this hard means most candidates never reach you. The ones who do have already done the job. And if a hire still isn't working, we step in and fix it rather than leaving it on you.
