Questions

Frequently Asked

Who manages my hire day to day? +

You handle the work. We handle everything around it, from payroll and compliance to performance support, through a dedicated account manager who is your single point of contact.

What do I actually get? +

One dedicated, full-time professional recruited for your role, on our payroll, working your hours and only for you. They speak fluent English and plug into your existing tools.

How involved do I have to be? +

Only where it counts. Most clients interview the finalists and approve the hire. We take care of the rest.

What if the hire doesn't work out? +

We recruit for the long term and stand behind the match. If something isn't working, we step in and fix it rather than leaving it on you.

How do we communicate? +

Slack, email, WhatsApp, or whatever your team already uses. Your hire works inside your day, not outside it.

Why not just hire abroad myself? +

You can, but it means building recruiting pipelines from scratch and managing foreign payroll and employment law on your own. We have already done that work, so you skip to the part that matters.
